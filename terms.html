Terms & Conditions

Terms and conditions for a safe, transparent learning experience at Jamia Rizvia Online Quran Academy.

Last updated: 12 May 2026

Eligibility

Students should join with honest details and a commitment to a respectful online learning environment.

  • Open to learners aged 6 and above
  • Guardian supervision is required for younger students
  • Accurate contact details are essential

Student Responsibilities

Students should arrive prepared, stay focused, and engage respectfully in every class.

  • Attend lessons on time with materials ready
  • Follow the teacher’s guidance and class etiquette
  • Communicate politely during lessons

Teacher Responsibilities

Teachers provide structured lessons, respectful online classrooms, and privacy-conscious support.

  • Deliver lesson plans tailored to each learner
  • Notify families of schedule changes promptly
  • Maintain a secure and respectful learning space

Online Class Rules

Virtual lessons should take place in a quiet, safe setting with reliable technology.

  • Use a stable connection and clear audio
  • Keep camera and microphone ready when requested
  • Avoid distractions and stay attentive

Attendance & Missed Classes

Regular attendance helps learners progress and supports consistent course delivery.

  • Inform the academy at least 24 hours before an absence
  • Missed lessons may be rescheduled where possible
  • Frequent absences may slow progress

Intellectual Property & Liability

All materials remain academy property and may not be shared without permission.

  • Course materials are protected by copyright
  • Materials may not be redistributed externally
  • The academy is not liable for third-party outages
