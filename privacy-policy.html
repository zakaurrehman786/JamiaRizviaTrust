Privacy Policy

How Jamia Rizvia Online Quran Academy collects and protects personal information for international students.

Last updated: 12 May 2026

What We Collect

We collect only the minimum information needed to manage lessons and keep learners safe online.

  • Student name, age, email, phone and country
  • Preferred course level and schedule availability
  • Guardian details for learners under 18

How We Collect Data

Data is gathered through trusted channels and only for booking, communication and lesson delivery.

  • Enrollment and contact forms submitted online
  • WhatsApp messages for class coordination
  • Emails to our official academy address

WhatsApp & Contact Forms

We use communication tools responsibly to ensure quick support and safe lesson management.

  • WhatsApp used for class updates and confirmations
  • Contact forms route enquiries directly to support
  • We do not share messages with advertisers

Cookies & Analytics

Cookies keep the site working well while analytics help improve the learning journey.

  • Essential cookies support navigation and security
  • Analytics cookies collect site-usage information to help us improve the website
  • We do not use cookies for personalised advertising

Children’s Privacy

Children’s information receives extra protection and is only processed with guardian approval.

  • Guardian consent is required for learners under 18
  • Child data supports safe, personalised lessons only
  • Teacher access is limited to necessary information

Data Protection & Rights

We secure your information and respond quickly to requests for access, correction or deletion.

  • Data is stored securely and accessed only by authorised staff
  • Students and guardians can request updates or deletion
  • Contact info@jamiarizviatrust.org with privacy requests
Privacy Support

Need help with your data?

Our team is here to answer privacy questions and respond to requests within 3–5 business days.
