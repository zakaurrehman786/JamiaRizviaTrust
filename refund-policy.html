Refund Policy

Clear refund terms for trial classes, monthly payments and online lesson cancellations.

Last updated: 12 May 2026

Free Trial Policy

Book one complimentary trial class to evaluate our tutors and learning style.

  • One free trial per student or family
  • Trial lessons are provided at no cost
  • Designed to help you choose the right course

Monthly Payments

Payments are processed after course confirmation and cover the chosen tuition package.

  • Payments collected in advance for each package
  • International payment support is available
  • Fee details are confirmed before the first paid lesson

Refund Eligibility

Refunds are reviewed individually in line with the selected plan and timing.

  • Refunds may apply if classes are cancelled pre-renewal
  • Trial lessons are not eligible for refunds
  • Requests are assessed fairly on a case-by-case basis

Student Cancellation

Provide advance notice so we can reschedule or support your learning plan.

  • Notify at least 24 hours before an absence
  • Late cancellations may be treated as attended lessons
  • Consistent attendance supports faster progress

Teacher Cancellation

Teacher cancellations are prioritised for rescheduling or refunding when needed.

  • Teacher cancellations receive priority rescheduling
  • Refunds are offered if a replacement lesson is unavailable
  • We aim to minimise disruption for learners

Technical Issues

Technical problems are reviewed promptly and resolved with fairness.

  • Report issues immediately for review
  • Approved refunds or make-up lessons are provided when needed
  • Requests are usually processed within 3–5 business days
Refund Support

Need help with a refund?

Our support team reviews every request fairly and aims to respond within 3–5 business days.
