Cookie Policy

Our approach to cookies and how you can manage them for a secure online learning experience.

Last updated: 12 May 2026

Essential Cookies

These cookies are required for secure site navigation and core features.

  • Support secure access to course information
  • Enable form submissions and session stability
  • Required for the website to function properly

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us improve the website without identifying individual users.

  • Track page visits and popular sections
  • Collect anonymous performance data only
  • Improve course registration and navigation

Marketing Cookies

Marketing cookies are active only with your explicit permission.

  • Require explicit consent before activation
  • Not used for third-party advertising without approval
  • Can be withdrawn at any time

Cookie Consent

Optional cookies are enabled only after you opt in.

  • Consent preferences are stored securely
  • You can update settings at any time
  • Essential cookies remain active for usability

Browser Settings

Your browser gives you direct control over cookies and privacy.

  • Block or delete cookies through your browser
  • Adjust settings per browsing session
  • Disabling analytics may affect some features

Privacy Support

We are happy to help if you have questions about cookies or consent.

  • Contact our support team for guidance
  • We do not share personal data without consent
  • Your privacy is important to our academy
Cookie Support

Need help managing cookies?

Our privacy team can help you adjust preferences and explain how cookies are used.
